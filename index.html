Benjamin Melavin

Graphic Designer - Artist

Hey, I'm Ben! I'm a graphic designer who proudly graduated with a BFA in Graphic Design from the University of Massachusetts Dartmouth in the spring of 2024.

Design has been apart of my life since high school — since then I've defined my style through the projects I embark on, both personal and professional. This very website is a byproduct of my studies, coded and designed by yours truly. While design is a big part of my life, it isn't my only interest. Art has been a part of my life much longer, and I continue to draw with just as much passion as when I started. When I have the time, I often try to draw as much as I can. I also play tabletop games such as Dungeons & Dragons with my peers. You may see that and more of my hobbies leak into the topics of my personal projects. Feel free to reach out if you're interested in my work, I would be more than happy to talk!
