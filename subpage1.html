Forged in Magic & Steel

Brand Design - Book Design - Product Design

Forged in Magic & Steel is not just a design project, but a collaboration and a product. As of spring of 2024, a sample of it was shown at the senior show at the College of Visual and Perfoming Arts building at UMass Dartmouth. The sample features the latest versions of the first two chapters of the planned six chapters intended for the final book.

The project started back in July of 2023 as a collection of house rules for a Dungeons & Dragons campaign I had hoped to host with a friend in the future. Over time, this expanded from a simple document to a lengthy collection of custom content. This ranged from new rules, new spells, to even new subclasses and entire systems. I enlisted the help of my friends, Ryan Dang, Frank Biancuzzo, and Brian McCormack. And later, the help of my partner, Jenna Bastien. Together not only did we write the content, but we tested all our material together to make sure it would function well in a proper tabletop session. We met almost every Thursday during the course of my spring semester, either brainstorming, writing, or playtesting.

The designs you see went through a few alterations throughout its development, based on my own tweaks and the feedback from peers and visiting professionals alike. I made many sketches and scribbles of potential page layouts, icons, and new ideas for material. From the start I wanted to do something simple, yet elegant. I also tried to kept a similar style inspired by the handbooks made official for D&D 5th edition, with my own creative liberties.
